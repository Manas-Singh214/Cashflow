Predictive Cash-Withdrawal & Cybercrime Analytics Pipeline

A. Project Summary & Core Analytical Goal
The primary objective of this project is to ingest, clean, standardize, and model multi-source banking, socio-economic, and cybercrime datasets across India. By doing so, the project establishes a robust analytical foundation to analyze the complex relationships between banking infrastructure (ATMs, PoS, Cards), socio-economic indicators (GDP, literacy, unemployment), and cyber/financial fraud incidents. The ultimate goal is to pave the way for proactive spatial-temporal hotspot forecasting and risk profiling of fraudulent cash-withdrawals.

B. Data Acquisition & Processing Strategy
- Target Sources:
  1. Indian Banks Financials: Yearly balance sheets containing deposits, advances, assets, net profit, NPA ratios, return on assets (ROA), and capital adequacy ratios.
  2. RBI ATM & Card Statistics: Monthly infrastructure metrics by bank, including on-site/off-site ATMs, PoS terminals, micro-ATMs, UPI QR codes, Bharat QR codes, and outstanding credit/debit cards.
  3. Bank Transactions: Individual transaction logs containing TransactionID, CustomerID, CustomerDOB, CustGender, CustLocation, CustAccountBalance, TransactionDate, TransactionTime, and TransactionAmount_INR.
  4. India Crime & Socio-Economic Data: District/state-level socio-economic indicators (GDP per capita, literacy, poverty, unemployment) combined with violent, property, and cybercrime indices.
  5. Parliament Session Reports: National macro trends of cyber incidents, defrauded amounts, and reported fraud volumes.
- Ingestion & Cleaning:
  - Standardizes bank names (e.g., mapping "State Bank of India LTD.", "STATE BANK OF INDIA", and "SBI" to a canonical name).
  - Normalizes state and union territory naming conventions using a predefined mapping (e.g., standardizing variations of "J&K" or "Daman & Diu").
  - Validates and parses datetime elements (DOBs, dates) and transaction amounts, dropping duplicates, null values, and summary rows (such as "ALL INDIA" or "TOTAL").

C. Python Processing & Specialized Engine Pipeline
- Core Processing Layer:
  - polars: Leveraged for high-throughput, multi-threaded columnar processing of high-volume datasets (e.g., cleaning and analyzing 1M+ bank transactions).
  - pandas & numpy: Used for standard DataFrame manipulation, formatting, and schema validation.
  - duckdb: An in-process SQL OLAP engine used to perform zero-overhead joins and windowed aggregations across bank financial datasets, transaction lists, and state-level crime indices.
- Geospatial & Spatial Indexing:
  - geopandas & shapely: Used to perform vector spatial manipulations and geographic boundary operations.
  - h3 (Uber H3): Computes global hierarchical hexagonal spatial indexes, allowing spatial binning of transaction amounts and crime occurrences to map risk densities.
  - osmnx: Models road networks and drive-time isochrones to assess bank-to-patrol dispatch and ATM accessibility.
  - pydeck & folium: Renders interactive, high-performance 3D spatial risk heatmaps and Leaflet-based density plots.
- NLP & Text Extraction:
  - spacy: Named Entity Recognition (NER) is used to extract geographic entities and crime modus operandi from unstructured incident notes and threat advisories.

D. Enterprise Data Warehousing (Star & Snowflake Schema)
To support multi-dimensional analytical queries in Business Intelligence tools, the normalized and merged data is modeled into a Star/Snowflake schema:
- Fact Tables:
  1. fact_bank_transactions:
     - Measures: TransactionAmount_INR, CustAccountBalance
     - Foreign Keys: customer_key, location_key, date_key, bank_key
  2. fact_bank_financials_annual:
     - Measures: Deposits_Cr, Advances_Cr, Total_Assets_Cr, Net_Profit_Cr, Gross_NPA_Cr, Net_NPA_Cr, Net_NPA_Pct, Return_on_Assets_Pct, Capital_Adequacy_Ratio_Pct, Credit_Deposit_Ratio, ATMs_Total, ATMs_OnSite, ATMs_OffSite, PoS_Terminals, Micro_ATMs, UPI_QR_Codes, Bharat_QR_Codes
     - Foreign Keys: bank_key, date_key
  3. fact_state_crime_socioeconomic:
     - Measures: Violent_Crimes, Property_Crimes, Cyber_Crimes, Total_Crimes_Reported, Crime_Rate_Per_100K, GDP_Per_Capita_INR, Literacy_Rate_Pct, Poverty_Rate_Pct, Unemployment_Rate_Pct, Cyber_Incidents_2016, Cyber_Incidents_2017, Cyber_Incidents_2018, Cyber_Crime_Rate_2018, Cyber_Fraud_Incidents, Cyber_Fraud_Amount_Reported_Lakh, Cyber_Fraud_Lien_Amount_Lakh, Cyber_Fraud_Refunded_Amount_Lakh
     - Foreign Keys: state_key, date_key
- Dimension Tables:
  - dim_bank: bank_key, bank_name, bank_type (Public, Private, Foreign)
  - dim_state: state_key, state_name, region_tier
  - dim_customer: customer_key, customer_id, gender, dob, age_group
  - dim_location: location_key, location_name (city/district), state_key, latitude, longitude, h3_cell_id
  - dim_date: date_key, full_date, day_of_week, month, year, quarter, is_month_end (crucial for detecting salary-cycle fraud surges)
- Storage & Partitioning: 
  - Outputs are serialized into daily/annual-partitioned .parquet format via pyarrow to support zero-latency analytical aggregations.

E. Business Intelligence & Exploratory Visualization Layer (Power BI, Tableau, hvPlot & PyGWalker)
- Power BI / Tableau Interactive Dashboards:
  - Socio-Economic vs Cybercrime Correlation: A dashboard displaying the correlation between literacy rates/GDP per capita and cybercrime rates. Includes drill-downs from national level to individual state/UT metrics.
  - Infrastructure Density & Fraud Overlays: Interactive GIS heatmaps displaying ATM, PoS, and QR code distributions, overlaid with hot zones showing high rates of cyber fraud incidents.
  - Banking Health & Risk Performance: Visualizes bank deposits, assets, Net Profits, and NPA percentages grouped by bank type.
  - Calculated Metrics (DAX / Tableau LOD):
    - DAX: Cyber Fraud Intensity = DIVIDE(SUM(fact_state_crime_socioeconomic[Cyber_Fraud_Amount_Reported_Lakh]), SUM(fact_state_crime_socioeconomic[GDP_Per_Capita_INR]))
    - Tableau LOD: { FIXED [State_UT] : SUM([ATMs_Total]) } / { FIXED [State_UT] : AVG([Population_Estimate]) } * 100000 (calculating ATM density per 100,000 citizens)
    - DAX: Bank NPA Risk = IF(fact_bank_financials_annual[Net_NPA_Pct] > 5.0, "Critical NPA Risk", "Stable NPA")
- Python Exploratory Visualization (EDA):
  - hvplot: Integrated within the Jupyter Notebook framework (`main.ipynb`) to create high-level interactive visualizations. Examples include:
    - Time-series line plots showing national cyber incident trends.
    - Scatter plots mapping transaction sizes against account balances with interactive tooltip details.
    - Categorical bar and box plots comparing transaction amounts across genders and age groups.
  - pygwalker: Embedded Tableau-like visual explorer inside Jupyter Notebooks. It permits fast drag-and-drop data visualization and prototyping on the processed bank transactions and crime datasets without writing extra boilerplate code.

F. Future ML & AI Evolution Roadmap
- Target Variables:
  - Predict the probability of a fraudulent cash withdrawal occurring in a specific H3 spatial cell.
  - Categorize bank transactions as normal, suspicious, or high-risk for cyber-fraud.
- Engineered Features:
  - Banking Penetration Index: Ratio of active cards, UPI QR codes, and ATMs to the state population.
  - Socio-Economic Deprivation Quotient: Combination of unemployment, literacy, and poverty rates.
  - Transaction Velocity & Outliers: Moving average of transaction frequency and deviations from normal balance-to-withdrawal ratios.
- Model Types: Spatio-temporal point-process models (e.g., self-exciting point processes), Random Forests, XGBoost, and neural network autoencoders for anomaly detection, feeding a future AI dispatch recommendation system for bank fraud mitigation.
